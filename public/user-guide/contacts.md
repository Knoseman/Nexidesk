# Contacts (CRM)

Go to **Contacts** in the left sidebar to manage your contact database.

Each contact holds:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Job title
- Company name

---

## Why use contacts?

Contacts help you build a picture of repeat customers. When you link a requester's email to a contact, their name and company appear in the ticket list and right rail for all future tickets from that address. It is like giving your inbox a memory.

---

## Creating a contact

1. Go to the **Contacts** page.
2. Click **New Contact**.
3. Fill in the fields. Email is required.
4. Click **Create**.

The contact is now available to link from any ticket.

---

## Editing a contact

1. Hover over the contact row and click the **pencil** icon that appears on the right.
2. The row expands into inline edit fields for Name, Company, Title, and Phone. The email address is shown but cannot be changed here.
3. Click the **green checkmark** to save, or the **grey ×** to cancel without saving.

---

## Deleting a contact

While editing a contact, a **red trash icon** appears below the cancel button, aligned to the right.

Click it to delete the contact. A confirmation prompt will ask you to confirm.

**What happens to linked tickets?**  
The contact record is removed, but all tickets from that email address remain. The CRM link (name, company, title, phone in the right rail) is cleared, but the ticket history is untouched.

This action cannot be undone.
