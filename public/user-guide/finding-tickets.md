# Finding Your Tickets

## Filtered Views

At the top of the left panel you will see a row of filters. These are the fastest way to cut through the noise and find exactly what you need.

| Filter | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **All** | Every ticket in the system |
| **New** | Unassigned tickets no one has touched yet |
| **My Open** | Tickets assigned to you that are being worked |
| **My Pending** | Tickets assigned to you that are waiting on someone else |
| **My Resolved** | Tickets assigned to you that you marked as resolved |
| **My Closed** | Tickets assigned to you that are finished |

**A solid daily routine:**
1. Start with **New** to pick up anything that arrived overnight.
2. Power through **My Open**.
3. Check **My Pending** to see if anything needs a gentle nudge.

## Searching

Click the magnifying glass at the top of the ticket list to search. The search looks through:

- Ticket subject lines
- All message text (both customer emails and your replies)
- Tag names
- Requester name, email, and company
- Assignee name and email

Results appear as you type — no submit button needed. The active filter still applies, so if you are in "My Open" and search, you will only see your own open tickets that match.

To go back to the full list, just clear the search field.

## Unread Badges

A **bold** ticket row means there is a message in that conversation you have not read yet. Opening the ticket marks it as read for you. This is personal — your colleagues will still see their own unread badges.
