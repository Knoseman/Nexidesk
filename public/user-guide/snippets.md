# Snippets

Go to **Snippets** in the left sidebar to manage your canned responses. These are your secret weapon for fast, consistent replies.

Each snippet has:
- A **name** (used for searching in the composer)
- A **body** (the text that gets inserted into your message)

---

## Writing good snippets

- Write them as mid-conversation replies.
- Do not include greetings like "Hi there" or sign-offs like "Best regards".
- Your email signature is added automatically to every reply.

**Pro tip:** The best snippets feel natural. Write them the way you actually talk to customers. All snippets are shared across the team, so keep them broadly useful.

---

## Using snippets in a ticket

1. Open the composer in any ticket.
2. Click the speech-bubble icon.
3. Search or browse for the snippet you need.
4. Click it to insert the text at your cursor.

That is it. Instant professionalism, zero typing fatigue.
