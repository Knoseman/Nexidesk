# Your Account

Click your name or avatar at the bottom of the left navigation to open your account settings. This is your space.

---

## What you can change

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Display name** | How your name appears to colleagues |
| **Phone number** | Your contact number |
| **Label colour** | The colour of your avatar in assignee dropdowns and the ticket list |
| **Email signature** | Appended automatically to every outbound reply |

---

## Email signature

The signature editor lets you add text, links, and basic styling with Bold, Italic, and Link. It is a lighter toolbar than the composer, but enough to make your signature yours.

Your signature is added to **replies only**. It is not added to internal notes — those stay clean and signature-free.
