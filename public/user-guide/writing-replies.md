# Writing Replies and Notes

The composer sits at the bottom of the ticket detail panel, ready when you are. It has two tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Reply** | Sends an email to the customer from the shared mailbox |
| **Note** | Adds an internal message only agents can see |

If you switch tabs with unsaved text, you will get a warning. Drafts are not saved if you reload the page, so try to finish your thought before refreshing.

---

## Formatting Your Message

The toolbar offers: **Bold**, *Italic*, Link, Bullet list, Code, and Code block.

**The Code button works two ways:**
- If you have text selected, it wraps it in inline `code`.
- If nothing is selected, it inserts a code block.

---

## More… (Subject, CC, BCC)

Click **More…** to expand additional reply fields. These only appear in **Reply** mode.

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Subject** | The email subject line. Pre-filled with the ticket subject. Edit it if you need to change the subject for this thread — it will update the ticket subject when the reply is sent. |
| **CC** | Extra recipients who receive the reply. Pre-filled from the message you are replying to. |
| **BCC** | Hidden recipients. Their address is not visible to anyone else. |

---

## Attachments

Click the paperclip to upload files. They go to storage straight away, and a pill appears showing the filename and size. Click the × to remove a file before sending.

You can attach multiple files. There is no hard size limit in the interface, but very large files may be rejected by the recipient's mail server.

---

## Snippets (Canned Responses)

Click the speech-bubble icon to open the snippets panel. Snippets are pre-written pieces of text you can drop into your message.

- **Global snippets** are available to everyone.
- **Personal snippets** are yours alone.

Clicking a snippet inserts it at your cursor. It does not replace what you have already written.

**Pro tip:** Write snippets as if they are mid-conversation replies. Do not include greetings or sign-offs — your email signature is added automatically.

To create or manage snippets, go to **Snippets** in the left-hand navigation.

---

## Sending

Click **Send** to submit your reply or note. For replies, the email joins the queue and is usually delivered within about 30 seconds. The button is disabled while sending is in progress.

After a successful send, the editor clears and the thread refreshes to show your new message. Done and dusted.
