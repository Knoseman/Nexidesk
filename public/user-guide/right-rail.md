# The Right-Hand Sidebar

The right rail gives you context about the ticket without cluttering the conversation. Think of it as the cheat sheet you always wish you had.

---

## Requester Details

This section shows:
- Email address
- Display name (from the CRM if linked, or from the email address)
- Company
- Phone number
- Job title

### Linking to a CRM contact

If the sender's email matches a contact record, it appears here automatically. If not, you can use the "Change requester" search box to find and link the right person.

This is useful when a customer emails from a personal address but their company record is under a different email.

---

## Tags

Tags are coloured labels you can apply to tickets. They appear in the list and are searchable.

Common uses: product area, issue type, escalation flag, or SLA tier.

### Adding a tag

1. Click the **Add tag** button in the Tags section.
2. Search for an existing tag or type a new name.
3. Press **Enter** to create a new tag with a default colour.

### Removing a tag

Click the **×** on the tag pill.

**Heads up:** Tags are shared across the whole team. Agree on a naming convention early — "billing", "Billing", and "billing-issue" are three separate tags. Inconsistent tags quickly become useless.

---

## Ticket Metadata

Below the requester and tags you will find:
- Ticket number
- Date created
- Channel (always "email" in the current version)
- Assigned agent
- Closed date (if the ticket is closed)
